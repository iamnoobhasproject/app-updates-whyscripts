BurnedWolf 4.6.0

BurnedCord global Push-to-Talk and voice cleanup update.

- Push-to-Talk is now global on Windows and keeps working while games or other applications are focused.
- The PTT listener does not reserve the selected key, so the foreground game still receives it normally.
- Mouse 4 and Mouse 5 can also be selected as Push-to-Talk buttons in the desktop client.
- Noise suppression now layers WebRTC echo/noise processing, RNNoise when available, voice-band filtering, light compression and an adaptive noise gate.
- Added Normal, Strong (default) and Very Strong noise profiles.
- Push-to-Talk bypasses the adaptive voice gate while held to avoid clipping quiet syllables while keeping denoising active.
- BurnedCord web renderer assets are cache-busted for this voice update.
